Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

At Vanya Living ("we", "our", or "us"), we deeply respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Because of how our site is designed, we do not store your customer data, purchase history, or personal records on any remote servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect or Store

Unlike traditional e-commerce platforms, Vanya Living operates on a direct-to-messaging model:

  • No Database Storage: We do not maintain a customer database, user accounts, or cloud storage of your personal details on our web servers.
  • No Payment Data Storage: We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank details, or financial credentials on our website.

2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device

To make your browsing and shopping experience seamless, your browser utilises local storage mechanisms (localStorage):

  • Shopping Cart & Preferences: Items you add to your cart and temporary form details (such as your name and delivery address) are stored strictly on your own device.
  • You maintain full control over this data: clearing your browser cache or clicking "Order" immediately clears your local cart.

3. Order Management via WhatsApp

  • How Orders Work: When you complete your cart or use the "Buy Now" feature, the site generates an itemised order summary containing your name, delivery address, and selected products.
  • Transmission: This information is sent directly via WhatsApp (wa.me/919982998476) to our business communication line.
  • WhatsApp's Privacy: Once sent, handling of your chat data is governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We use these chats solely to fulfil your delivery and provide customer support.

4. Communications and Form Submissions

If you reach out through our "Reach Us" section:

  • Your input is transmitted via your device's default email client (mailto:vanya.living.care@gmail.com).
  • We use this correspondence exclusively to evaluate your proposal or coordinate partnerships. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your email to third parties.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics pixels, or behavioural profiling cookies. Local storage is used solely for technical functionality.

6. Data Security

Because customer records are handled peer-to-peer through encrypted WhatsApp channels rather than stored on web databases, your exposure to remote data breaches is minimised.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
